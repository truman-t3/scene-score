# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

Scene Score welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people of every background.

- Discuss work and ideas, not personal traits.
- Give visual feedback with specific, actionable observations.
- Do not share images, personal data, or private project material without permission.
- Do not use the project to harass, discriminate against, or impersonate others.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation that violates these guidelines. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive public content.
