## Stability claim / 稳定性说明

Gallery Score stabilizes source-to-mark hierarchy: large factual rest, compact anchor, one hero rhythm, one phrase, and at most one accent. It does not promise identical pixels, crop, or field edge. A run fails when this hierarchy is absent, even if its colours are attractive.

Gallery Score 稳定的是“来自原图的大留白、紧凑主体、一个主节拍、一条方向线、最多一个重音”的层级，不承诺相同像素、裁剪或场形。只要层级缺失，即使颜色好看也视为失败。
