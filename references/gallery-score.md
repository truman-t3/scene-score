# Gallery Score / 画廊式场景乐谱

Use this optional composition family when the user asks for the showcased visual language: **large factual breathing space, one distilled photographic anchor, and a small number of source-derived rhythmic marks.** It makes the design language repeatable without turning every result into the same poster.

当用户明确希望使用案例中的“大留白、提炼主体、少量强节奏”语言时使用。本参考让视觉层级稳定，但不把所有照片做成同一版式。

## Stable hierarchy / 稳定层级

Build a compact visual contract before generating:

```text
Anchor: <the retained subject or scene relationship>
Quiet field: <a source-derived wall / fog / sky / tabletop / shadow>
Hero rhythm: <one repeated interval or projected plane from the source>
Phrase: <one direction from route / rail / light / shadow / horizon>
Accent: <one small source colour or event>
```

- Let the **quiet field** occupy roughly 40–60% of the frame. It must grow from a factual low-information zone, not an arbitrary cream backdrop.
- Keep the **photographic anchor** compact and readable, normally about 25–45% of the frame. It can be cropped or absorbed at one edge, but may not become a pasted sticker.
- Use **one hero rhythm** only: a crossing interval, rising step mass, window-light shaft, canopy density, or a product's bottle-and-shadow axis.
- Use **one phrase** only: a fine route, rail, projected-light direction, shadow, or horizon. It should connect to the anchor or hero rhythm.
- Use **one accent** only, taken from the source. If the image works without it, omit it.
- Stop after these elements are clear. Do not fill the remaining space with extra circles, stripes, labels, or texture.

For an **expressive Gallery Score**, the re-authored field and score marks together must visibly reorganize at least half the frame. A restrained result can still fail if it leaves the photograph nearly intact.

## Source-specific routes / 从场景出发

Choose the structure that the source already offers:

| Source condition | Gallery Score move |
| --- | --- |
| Rain street or crossing | Keep the walker/street as anchor; extend unequal crossing bands as the hero rhythm; use a single route line toward the bus; let mist become the rest. |
| Stairs or architecture | Keep the person and stair rise; simplify selected treads into an ascending mass; let the handrail continue as the only phrase; preserve wall or sky as the rest. |
| Window-lit product or still life | Keep the product and its cast shadow; expand the window shaft into translucent vertical planes; reserve tabletop or wall as rest; use the product colour as one accent. |
| Water, fog, trees, reeds | Keep a trunk, boat, or horizon; use density shifts rather than equal bars; let fog or water carry the quiet field. |

These are source routes, not templates. Change the field edge, placement, interval count, and palette whenever the source changes.

If the source contains a genuinely circular stopper, lamp, reflection, or opening, one enlarged translucent disc may act as the accent mass. If it does not, do not introduce a disc.

## Prompt clause / 提示词补充句

Add this after the scene contract when the user requests Gallery Score:

```text
Use the Gallery Score composition family: reserve a large source-derived quiet field; keep one compact factual photographic anchor; translate only one dominant source rhythm and one directional phrase; use at most one small accent. The result should feel like a distilled exhibition print with clear breathing room, not a full-frame photo filter or a generic poster recipe.
```

## Acceptance / 验收

The result is accepted only if, at thumbnail size:

- the quiet field is visibly present but clearly traceable to the source;
- the anchor, hero rhythm, and phrase form one hierarchy;
- at least two structural source-derived changes survive;
- in expressive mode, the score fields visibly reorganize at least half the frame rather than merely tinting the photo;
- removing the source photo would make the chosen rhythm harder to justify.

Image generation remains non-deterministic: this mode stabilizes hierarchy, scene-to-mark translation, and restraint—not exact pixels, crop, or a fixed shape. If the first result misses the hierarchy, revise that one failed role rather than regenerating randomly.
